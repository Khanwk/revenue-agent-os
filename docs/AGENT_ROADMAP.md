# Agent roadmap

Already implemented:
1. Scout — finds and ranks opportunities.
2. Pitch — writes truthful, project-specific proposal drafts.
3. Architect — creates a delivery plan.

Recommended next agents:
4. Discovery — turns client messages/calls into confirmed requirements and unanswered questions.
5. Estimator — produces effort ranges, milestones, dependencies and commercial risk.
6. Follow-up — prepares human-approved follow-ups for open opportunities.
7. QA — checks deliverables against project requirements before client delivery.
8. Knowledge — answers team questions from company standards and project knowledge.

Do not create autonomous bid submission until marketplace rules, reliability and human approval controls are proven.
