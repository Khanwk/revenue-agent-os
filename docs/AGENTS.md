# Agent map

| Category | Agent | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | Scout | Find and rank freelance projects against company skills |
| Sales | Pitch | Produce a truthful tailored proposal |
| Sales | Prospector | Build lead/ICP research plans |
| Sales | Reach | Prepare personalized outreach and follow-ups |
| Sales | Discover | Prepare discovery and expose scope gaps |
| Sales | Estimate | Produce effort/pricing assumptions and milestones |
| Delivery | Architect | Convert opportunity into delivery architecture and phases |
| Delivery | Spec | Create requirements and acceptance criteria |
| Delivery | Guardian | QA/release/handover planning |
| Growth | Growth | Marketing and acquisition planning |
| Growth | Expand | Retention, upsell and referrals |
| Growth | Proof | Turn finished work into case studies |
| Operations | Ops | SOPs and delegation processes |
| Operations | Advisor | Structured founder/company decisions |

The generic agents share the same authenticated runner and structured output contract. Scout, Pitch and Architect have specialized schemas/UI because they are core revenue workflows.
